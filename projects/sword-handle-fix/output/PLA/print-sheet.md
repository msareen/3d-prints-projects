## Part notes: sword-handle-fix (pommel cap)

**Plate:** the 3MF holds 2 caps side by side, each with the closed end down and the open rim up. They need no supports and no brim.

**Accepted warning:** `small_holes`, the 3 mm vent hole in the closed end. It prints slightly undersize, which is fine for a vent. Open it with a 3 mm drill bit by hand if it closes up.

**Fit and gluing:**
1. Dry-fit first. The bore is straight at the handle's largest size (32.2 × 28.9, which includes 0.3 mm of glue gap per side), so the cap should slide fully over the broken end.
2. The handle is thinner near the break (about 20 mm thick), so the gap on the narrow faces can be several mm. Use a **gap-filling glue**: 2-part epoxy (best) or hot glue. Thin superglue will not bridge the gap.
3. Put glue inside the cap and on the handle, push the cap on until the broken edge reaches the bottom, and wipe off what squeezes out of the vent and the rim. Line up the chevrons with the handle's chevron face. Leave epoxy to cure for its full time before playing.

**Looks:** print in black PLA to match the handle. The chevron grooves look best with outer walls at 40 mm/s or slower.

## PLA handling

- Dry the filament if it pops or strings (50 °C for 4–6 h).
- Do not leave PLA parts in a hot car or in direct sun: they soften and creep.

Slicing and printing are your steps. Do not leave the printer unattended.
