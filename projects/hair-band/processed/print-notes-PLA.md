**PLA warning.** PLA is about 1.5x stiffer than PETG but more brittle. With the rev 4 spine, opening the band over a head strains it well below PLA's limit. Still, do not open it wider than needed: a crack starts on the arms, about a third of the way from the tips. Never leave it in a hot car (PLA softens at about 55 °C and takes a new shape). For daily wear, PETG is the safer choice. Choose PLA if the PETG band still feels too soft.
